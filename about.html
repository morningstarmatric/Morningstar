About Morning Star Matriculation School

Building character, curiosity, and confidence, one child at a time.

Our Story

Founded on a simple belief

Morning Star Matriculation School carries forward a proud 69-year legacy of educational service. The school began its new academic journey on 10th June 2024 with a small group of students and a clear vision: quality, value-based education in a safe and nurturing environment.

In the years since, we have grown quickly, adding the KVB Block, a Smart Classroom, a 40-system Computer Laboratory, a Library and indoor games and play areas, while students earned prizes and medals in chess, yoga, KHO-KHO, drawing, science, quiz, literary, cultural and sports events.

Today we are building a future-ready school, with a focus on robotics, artificial intelligence, innovation and digital learning, alongside strong values, discipline and community service, nurturing every child to learn, grow, innovate, inspire and achieve.
