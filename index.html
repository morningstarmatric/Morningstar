Our Achievements

Established in 2024, Morning Star Matriculation School has made remarkable progress in a short span of two years, creating opportunities for students to excel in academics, sports, yoga, karate, arts and co-curricular activities.

Our students have participated in various competitions and earned special recognition at the District Level in areas such as Yoga, Karate, Drawing and other talent-based competitions.

A New Chapter in Sports · 2026–27

The 2026–27 academic year marked a significant milestone in the sporting journey of our school.

With the appointment of a dedicated Physical Education Teacher, our students received systematic training and actively participated in Zonal-level sports competitions, gaining valuable exposure and experience. Adding to this, the school successfully organised a grand Annual Sports Day — ATHLETINA 2K26 — giving students a vibrant platform to demonstrate their talent, discipline, teamwork, determination and sportsmanship.

These accomplishments reflect our commitment to the holistic development of every child. From a new beginning in 2024 to a growing legacy of achievement, Morning Star continues to move forward with confidence and purpose.
